# Part 107 Study Tracker — [Your Name]

## Study rules (set [Start Date])
- **Project instructions are the source of truth.** This section mirrors the "Part 107 Master Ground Instructor" instructions.
- **Pre-staging:** The lesson page (theory, SVGs, live weather, videos) is fully built and published before the daily start time. On "start", begin teaching immediately.
- **Gagné's Nine Events, 90 min max:** 
  - Phase 1: Recall & Remediation 0–10 min (missed bank first; concepts missed more than once get a whiteboard-style SVG breakdown first). 
  - Phase 2: Concepts 10–50 min, with a **hard stop**: plates that don't fit roll to the next day. 
  - Phase 3: Quiz 50–90 min, 20 questions (14 new + 6 review), pass at 18/20.
- **Scenarios:** Framed around flying a **[Your Drone Model]** in **[Your Local Area / Airport]** airspace, using live METAR/TAF and current UAS Facility Maps. Drone specs are verified, never guessed.
- **Tactile charts:** Print FAA-CT-8080-2H sectional excerpts (Figs. 20–26, 69–71, Legend 1) for chart lessons.
- **Images:** Only public-domain Testing Supplement figures plus original SVGs. Kindle books are used only for structure and pacing.
- **Daily session:** Starts at [Your Preferred Time], **Monday through Saturday**. 
- **Sunday is an off day — no session.**
- **Session format:** 1 hour 30 minutes = 10 min remediation + 40 min concepts + 40 min practice questions.
- **Concepts:** Combine written theory with high-quality graphics. Each concept gets a rendered graphic AND written explanation (what it is, why, how to answer the test question, which rule applies). Visuals alone are not enough. 
- **Video for every concept:** Suggest one online video (e.g. YouTube) per concept, in English. Only use links found by search, never invented. 
- **Dedicated graphical lessons for METAR (Lesson 15) and TAF (Lesson 16)** — visual decoding with written theory.
- **Real-world chart practice:** Exercises on the local airspace in chart and airspace lessons, plus a dedicated workshop (Lesson 17). Altitudes and frequencies are always checked against the current sectional and FAA UAS Facility Maps. 
- **Practice:** 20 questions (14 from the day's lesson + 6 review, 70/30). Missed-bank questions come first. Base questions on Testing Supplement figures where possible. Chart questions always show a graphic.
- **Lesson passed:** 90% or higher (18/20). Below 90% = repeat the lesson next session.
- **Final assessment:** After all lessons, full mock tests of 60 questions in 2 hours. **Benchmark: 85% or higher on two consecutive mocks.**
- **Administrative milestone:** Create an FTN in IACRA and schedule the exam at a PSI testing center to set a firm deadline.
